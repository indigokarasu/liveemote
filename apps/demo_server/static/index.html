LiveEmote — affect-keyed live avatar

Real LLM-driven avatar with its own face and voice. Your webcam is read as a focus + energy signal, never as a face to copy or swap. The avatar mirrors/reflects your affect (delayed, damped, never literal) and speaks back through the configured LLM with structured voice + emotion tags.

Webcam (signal source)

Frames stay local. The browser streams downsampled JPEGs to the server-side perception tracker; the avatar never displays your face.
